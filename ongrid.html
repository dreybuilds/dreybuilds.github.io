on-grid · embedded systems and blockchain

energy readings from device to smart contract

As technical co-founder, I designed and built a system to measure and validate power consumption using low-cost hardware for sites with intermittent connectivity.

Project images →

At the edge: I built ESP32-S3 and Raspberry Pi nodes for data logging and verification across peer-to-peer networks using Rust/libp2p.

Across the system: I designed the off-chain and on-chain architecture, integrated backend data collection with smart contract ABIs, and collaborated with smart contract and full-stack developers.

From prototype to hardware: I coordinated with fabricators on technical specifications, manufacturability, and the transition toward production. We stored readings in InfluxDB and plotted them in Grafana to see how consumption varied over time and spot unusual patterns.
